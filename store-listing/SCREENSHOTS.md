# Screenshot plan (App Store — 1600 × 900 px each, min 3)

Screenshots are the #1 thing merchants judge. Show the value, not the UI chrome.
Take each at 1600×900 (or larger 16:9 and downscale). A thin caption baked into
the top of each image helps.

1. **The widget on a real product page** (hero shot).
   Caption: "Show an accurate delivery date on every product page."
   - A clean product page with the block visible near the price/buy button:
     🚚 *Order today, get it by Friday, 18 July.*

2. **Before / after** (conversion angle).
   Caption: "Turn delivery doubt into a reason to buy."
   - Split image: left = product page without a date; right = same page with the
     "Get it by" message highlighted.

3. **The block settings in the theme editor** (ease-of-setup angle).
   Caption: "Set your cutoff, shipping time and holidays — no code."
   - Theme editor open with the Delivery Date block settings panel showing
     preparation time, cutoff time, shipping min/max, dispatch days, holidays.

4. **Accuracy angle** (the wedge) — optional but strong.
   Caption: "Weekends, holidays and cutoff times — always correct."
   - Small visual: a calendar with weekend/holiday days crossed out and the
     computed dispatch/delivery days marked.

5. **Mobile view** — optional.
   Caption: "Looks great on mobile."
   - The widget on a product page in a phone frame.

Tips:
- Use your dev store (`test-store-piomsfsf`) with a nice demo product + image.
- Keep the message text realistic and readable.
- Consistent background/brand color across all shots reads as "polished".

## Full description  (long-form; keyword-rich, benefit-first)

Give shoppers the confidence to buy by showing a clear, accurate **estimated delivery date** right on your product pages — like *"Order today, get it by Friday, 18 July."*

Uncertainty about delivery is one of the biggest reasons shoppers abandon their carts. A precise "Get it by" date builds trust, increases conversions, and cuts down on "where is my order?" support tickets.

**Why Estimated Delivery Date is different**

- **Actually accurate.** Most delivery-date apps get the math wrong on weekends, holidays, and order cutoff times. Our engine handles all of it correctly, in your shop's timezone — so a Saturday order never shows an impossible dispatch date.
- **Never breaks.** The date is calculated in the shopper's browser, so the widget loads instantly and keeps working even if anything else is down. No slow product pages, no missing dates during your busiest sale.
- **Set up in 2 minutes.** Add the block to your product template in the theme editor — no code and no developer required.
- **Fully customizable.** Set your preparation time, order cutoff, shipping window, dispatch days, and holidays. Style the message to match your theme.

**Features**

- Accurate "Get it by" date or delivery range on every product page
- Timezone-aware order **cutoff** ("order before 2pm" logic)
- **Weekend and holiday** aware — skips days you don't ship
- Custom **preparation** time and **shipping** window
- Choose exactly which days you dispatch
- Message text, weekday, icon, color and alignment all configurable
- Works on any Online Store 2.0 theme

**Free to install.**
